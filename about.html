About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is imagined as a neighborhood gathering place where warm bread and friendly service make each visit feel personal. Our focus is simple: bake carefully and serve our community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We value simple ingredients, local grains when available, and patient baking methods that bring out natural flavor.
